The western Lägernkopf is one of the warmest and driest places to live in Baden. That is appreciated not only by people. Many rare plants and animals also find a sunny habitat here.

Between limestone rocks, dry meadows, scree and blackthorn scrub, the blue-violet **European Michaelmas daisy** (*Aster amellus*) flowers in late summer, for example. At the foot of the rock slabs stand the tall, lilac flower heads of the **mountain chives** (*Allium senescens*). It is a close relative of chives.

Many insects and reptiles like the hot south-facing slope of the Limmat gorge. These include colourful butterflies – among them four species of **blues** –, rare grasshoppers and the **sand lizard**. The increasingly rare **smooth snake** also occurs here. It is harmless to humans.
