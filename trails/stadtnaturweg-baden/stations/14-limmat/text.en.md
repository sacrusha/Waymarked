The White-throated Dipper, the White Wagtail and the Grey Wagtail like to live by clear, fast-flowing streams and rivers. On the Limmat you can watch them all year round. In winter there are often even more of them, because birds from colder regions also come here to look for food.

The **White-throated Dipper** is the only native songbird that can swim and dive. It looks for its food on the bed of the water, where it finds insect larvae and other small aquatic animals. Although it looks similar to the “ordinary” blackbird, it is not related to it. The large white patch on its breast is typical.

The **wagtails** are dainty birds with strikingly long tail feathers. When they are not flying, they often bob their tails. The Grey Wagtail can be recognised by its yellow underside. It likes to sit on stones in the water or on roofs close to the river.
