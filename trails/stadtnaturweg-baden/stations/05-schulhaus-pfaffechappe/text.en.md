When the Tannegg school complex was renovated in 2021, the flat roof was greened and fitted with a solar array. Mainly low plants grow around the solar modules. That is intentional: the soil there contains few nutrients so that the plants stay small and do not shade the modules.

In the middle of the roof, by contrast, native perennials and larger wild shrubs grow. Deadwood structures have also been created. They contribute to biodiversity and offer insects and small animals food and hiding places.

The green roof links the two valuable limestone habitats of Schartenfels and the Stein ruins. Bees, butterflies and other insects can use it as an ecological “stepping stone” to get from one habitat to the other.
