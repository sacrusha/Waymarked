A surprising number of bird species live in the Stadtturm. Every April the **Alpine Swifts** arrive here from their winter quarters in Africa. They breed in the attic and raise their young.

The **Common Kestrel** has also lived in the tower for many years. It likes to breed in one of the nest boxes behind the arrow slits.

For some years now, **Goosanders** have been competing with the kestrel for the space. Getting its young safely down to the Limmat is a great challenge for this waterbird, because the chicks first have to drop 20 metres onto the street.

**Jackdaws** have recently moved into the oriels. At first glance they resemble carrion crows, but they are easy to tell apart by their pale grey nape.
