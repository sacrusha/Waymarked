When the **Alpine Swifts** circle the Landvogteischloss for the first time each year, spring has arrived. You can recognise them by their white bellies and their loud trilling calls. These long-distance migrants come to Baden from Africa in April to breed. By September they are already heading back to their winter quarters.

High above the roofs of our old town, Alpine Swifts hunt insects, reaching more than 100 kilometres per hour. Unlike swallows, they stay in the air almost without a break during the day.

The colony in the Landvogteischloss numbers around 50 breeding pairs. It is one of the largest on the Swiss Plateau. That is why the Alpine Swift is also the “emblem bird” of Baden’s nature and bird protection society, NAVO.
