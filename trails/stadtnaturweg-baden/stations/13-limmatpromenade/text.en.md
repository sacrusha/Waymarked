The majestic **plane trees**, now over 40 metres tall, were planted in 1830 when the Limmatpromenade was laid out. The popular path allowed spa guests then, as now, a picturesque stroll from the spa district to the lower old town. In search of sunlight, the plane trees grew towards the Limmat and leaned over the water.

In autumn and winter the trees bear decorative brown fruit clusters that hang from the branches like Christmas baubles. They are easy to see from the lift.

The bark of the plane tree peels off in plates every year. As a result, few insects can settle on the trunk. In return, the many tree hollows offer valuable hiding places. Hole-nesting birds, hornets and wild bees find shelter here. The Common Noctule, a species of bat, can also be found here. It likes to hunt along rivers.
