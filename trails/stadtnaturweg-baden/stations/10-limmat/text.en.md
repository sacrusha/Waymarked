The **Goosander** is larger than most of the duck species found here. It is a skilful fish hunter. Its slender, hooked bill has fine tooth-like serrations along the edge that help it grip slippery prey.

Originally the Goosander was found mainly in northern Europe. Today it can also be seen on Swiss lakes and along the Limmat in Baden. There it dives for fish or rests on stones in the river. The male can be recognised by its glossy dark green head and neck.

Goosanders breed in cavities. They use natural tree hollows, rock niches and sometimes buildings. One pair, for example, breeds high up in Baden’s Stadtturm.
