Perhaps you will spot a **common wall lizard** venturing out of a crevice. It is looking for warmth. Like all reptiles, it cannot keep its body temperature constant by itself. Only in the sun does it reach “operating temperature” and become quick and agile. That is why it likes sunlit walls, stones and scree fields.

The wall lizard’s home lies south of the Alps. It probably spread northwards with freight traffic.

The **sand lizard** is related to the wall lizard. It looks sturdier and stockier. The males stand out with their green belly colouring. The sand lizard likes dry, sunny places with low plants.
